Flashing Instructions

Please follow the steps carefully to avoid soft-bricking your device. Make sure you back up critical data before unlocking the bootloader.

Warning Disclaimer

Flashing custom firmware voids your warranty. We are not responsible for bricked phones, lost data, or hardware damage.

Installation Guide

1

Verify you are on the latest Stock firmware (Check via SamFW) and ensure bootloader is unlocked.

2

Flash OFOX/TWRP and flash your patched vbmeta using Odin with Auto Reboot unticked.

3

Reboot into custom recovery, flash repartition.zip, restart recovery, then wipe data & system partitions [Repartition for A127X].

4

Sideload or install the ROM zip, format data, then reboot system!
